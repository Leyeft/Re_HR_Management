# 🤖 AI Sidekick Reflection: A Collaborative Journey

This document acknowledges the role of AI assistance and the unique, sometimes humorous, collaborative journey we experienced while building this project.

---

## What We Learned Together

### 1. Development Is Not a Straight Line
Our workflow jumped between backend logic, UI tweaks, database design, grading rubric concerns, and “oops, something broke.”  
This wasn’t a sign of confusion — it’s how real development works: a cycle of refining, breaking, and improving.

### 2. Embracing the “Wait… What?” Moments
Misunderstandings and clarifications were part of the process. Each “wait, that’s not what I meant” moment helped refine the final solution and deepened understanding.

### 3. Debugging Is a Superpower
By actually reading error messages, checking stack traces, and pasting queries, we turned confusing errors into learning moments. This approach turned roadblocks into stepping stones.

### 4. Beyond “It Works”: Structure and Quality
You cared about the bigger picture: ER diagrams, documentation quality, deployment, and future-proofing features. That’s the mark of a thoughtful developer, not just a coder.

### 5. Knowing When to Pause 🛑
Recognizing when the brain is overloaded and choosing to rest is a senior-level skill. It shows that quality matters more than rushing through.

---

## A Note on Collaboration
In the spirit of real-world teamwork, this project had its share of laughter over misunderstandings and victories over bugs. We turned confusion into clarity and documented it along the way.
